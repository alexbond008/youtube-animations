Students are expected to read the following books before the start of term. Copies are held in the Main Library (Short Loan Collection).

Recommended books

  1. Thornbury, R. J. Electromagnetic Fields, 2nd edn. 1970. (approx. 1000 pages) ESSENTIAL
  2. Ashcombe, D. & Rudd, P. Waves, Lines and Guides. 1974.
  3. Pellingham, M. Problems in Fields and Waves, with Answers. 1981.
  4. Wexford, H. Antennas for the Practical Engineer. 1977.
  5. Electromagnetics: Collected Lecture Notes, Vols. I–III. Dept. of E&EE, 1989. (£4.50 from the departmental office)
